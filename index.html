Audit any Google Business Profile in three clicks

MapGauge scores a business’s profile on Google Maps from 0 to 100 and gives you a to-do list, most important fixes first. No account, nothing to set up.

For local SEO specialists, agencies and freelancers, and owners checking their own listing.

How it works

MapGauge works inside Google Maps, on the pages you already use.

  1. 1Search on Google Maps

    Search for any type of business, like “plumber london”. The first 20 results get a badge with their primary category, rating and review count.

  2. 2Open a business

    Click a business. MapGauge’s box appears at the bottom-right of its panel, with the Audit button.

  3. 3Click Audit

    A report opens in a new tab: the score, the to-do list, categories, attributes, reviews, posts, and a comparison with the top 10 results of your search.

What the audit checks

11 checks, each with where to fix it in the Business Profile (for example: Edit profile → Hours).

  • ClaimedDoes an owner manage the profile?
  • ReviewsAt least 10 reviews?
  • Rating4.0 or higher?
  • Owner repliesAre at least half of the reviews Google shows answered?
  • PostsHas the owner posted in the last 30 days?
  • Opening hoursAre they listed?
  • WebsiteIs one listed?
  • PhoneIs a number listed?
  • PhotosAt least 10?
  • DescriptionHas the owner written one?
  • CategoriesAre there secondary categories for the business’s other services?

Hotels can’t set opening hours or write their own description, so those checks are skipped for them. If Google’s data can’t be read in full, MapGauge says “Data may be incomplete” instead of showing a wrong number.

More than a score

Everything below is free.

  • Categories

    See a business’s primary and secondary categories on every result, and search all 4,081 Business Profile categories in the report.

  • Compare with competitors

    The business next to the top 10 results of the search you opened it from, and where it falls behind. 3 compares a day.

  • Badges on Google Search

    The map box on a Google search gets the same badges, and an “Audit on Maps” button in the business panel.

Private by design

  • MapGauge Free sends nothing to any server. No account, no analytics, no tracking.
  • It reads only what Google Maps or Google Search has already loaded for you. It makes no searches you didn’t start, and doesn’t scroll or click for you.
  • Reports are saved only in your browser and are deleted when you remove the extension.
