Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026 · applies to the MapGauge Chrome extension, Free and Pro plans

MapGauge Free sends nothing to any server. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the extension does happens inside your own browser, and your audit reports stay on your own computer. With Pro, the one exception is the licence check with Gumroad, described below.

What MapGauge reads

MapGauge works only on Google Maps pages (https://www.google.com/maps…) and Google Search results pages (https://www.google.com/search…). There it reads the data that Google has already loaded for you:

  • Search results: the businesses in the results list of a search you made, to show badges with their category, rating and review count, to compare a business you audit with the top results of that search and, with Pro, to filter them by website, phone, claimed status and review count (the lead filters). Filter choices are kept only while the Maps tab is open.
  • Google Search: on a search you made, the businesses in the map box and on its "More places" page (name, primary category, rating, review count and Google's IDs for them), to show the same badges. Nothing from Google Search is saved.
  • The business panel: the public details of a business you opened (name, categories, rating, review count, address, website, phone, opening hours, photo count, description, attributes such as accessibility or payment options, whether it is a hotel, whether Google shows it as unclaimed, and Google's IDs for it), to build the audit. Also its star breakdown (how many 1★ to 5★ reviews) and, for the few reviews Google loads with the business, only each review's date, star rating and whether the owner replied. MapGauge never uses or keeps reviewers' names, photos or review text. For the owner's newest posts Google loads with the business (up to 10), only each post's date, type (update or event) and button label, never its text, photos or links.
  • The Reviews tab: if you open a business's Reviews tab and scroll it yourself, the reviews Google loads for you there: only each review's date, last edit date and star rating, whether the owner replied and when. MapGauge never uses or keeps reviewers' names, photos or review text, or Google's ID for the review. It shows how many it has under the Audit button. It also notes whether you picked a topic or typed a word in that tab's search box, only to tell that the list is filtered; the topic and the word are never kept.

MapGauge sends no extra requests to Google. It makes no searches you didn't start, doesn't scroll or click for you, and never changes what Google shows. It doesn't read your Google account, your email, your location history or any other website.

While a Maps or Search tab is open, this data is kept only in that tab's memory. It is gone when you close or reload the tab, unless you save an audit report.

What MapGauge stores

When you click Audit on a business, MapGauge saves an audit report. The toolbar popup shows your most recent reports from the same list.

  • What a report holds: the business's public details listed above, the time you saved it, and the address of the Google Maps page you were on (so you can reopen it). That address can include your search words and the map area you were looking at.
  • For the Compare tab: if you opened the business from a search, the report also keeps your search words, the business's position in that search, and for the first 10 other businesses in the results their public name, categories, rating, review count, photo count, opening hours, website, phone, whether it is a hotel, whether Google shows it as unclaimed and its Google ID.
  • For the review audit: if you loaded reviews on the business's Reviews tab before clicking Audit, the report also keeps up to 1,000 of them, each with only the dates, star rating and whether the owner replied listed above.
  • Compares used today: the date and how many compares you used on it (Free includes 3 a day). This count stays on your computer.
  • Pro licence (only if you activate Pro): your licence key, whether Gumroad said it is active (or why not, for example "refunded"), and when it was last checked. Removing the key on MapGauge's Pro licence page deletes it.
  • PDF branding (Pro, only if you set it): the logo picture, company name and contact line you enter on MapGauge's Pro page, to print them at the top of your PDF reports. They are never sent anywhere; Clear all on that page deletes them.
  • Where: in chrome.storage.local, the extension's own storage on your computer. It isn't synced to your Google account or sent anywhere.
  • How many: at most 50 reports. Auditing the same business again replaces its old report, and the oldest report is removed when a new one would go past 50.

How to delete them: remove the extension. Open chrome://extensions, find MapGauge and click Remove (or right-click the MapGauge icon in the toolbar and choose Remove from Chrome). Chrome then deletes all of MapGauge's saved data. If you install it again, it starts empty.

Permissions and why

storageTo save your audit reports, and how many compares you used today, on your own computer.
Content script on https://www.google.com/maps, https://www.google.com/maps/* and https://www.google.com/maps?*To read the Maps data described above and show badges and the Audit button.
Content script on https://www.google.com/search*To read the businesses of the map box on Google Search results, described above, and show badges on them. It runs on Google Maps and Google Search results only, on no other site.
https://api.gumroad.com/*, optionalOnly for Pro: to check your licence key with Gumroad. Chrome asks for it when you click Activate, never before, and MapGauge gives it back when you remove your key. Free users never grant it.

MapGauge asks for nothing else: no access to all websites, your tabs list, your browsing history, cookies or downloads.

Every network request MapGauge makes

PlanRequests
FreeNone. All code, icons and data ship inside the extension. Nothing is downloaded or uploaded.
ProOne kind: POST https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify, which sends Gumroad your licence key and MapGauge's Pro product ID, nothing else (no reports, no businesses, no browsing data). It is sent when you click Activate or Check now, and at most once a day while you use MapGauge (when Chrome starts or when you click Audit), so a cancelled or refunded licence stops unlocking Pro. Gumroad answers whether the licence is active; its privacy policy applies to the request.

Three things that are not MapGauge requests, listed so the picture is complete: the "Open Google Maps", "Open on Google Maps" and "Audit on Maps" links open a normal Google Maps tab only when you click them (Google's own privacy policy applies there), the "Join early access" and "Get Pro" buttons on locked Pro cards open Gumroad's page in a new tab only when you click them (Gumroad's privacy policy applies there; MapGauge sends Gumroad nothing), and Chrome itself checks the Chrome Web Store for extension updates.

Sharing and selling

MapGauge collects no personal data, so there is nothing to sell, share or transfer. Nobody, including us, can see your reports.

The Pro plan

Payments go through Gumroad only, and MapGauge never sees your card details. Pro buttons in the extension open Gumroad's page in a new tab only when you click them; anything you enter there, such as your email, goes to Gumroad under Gumroad's privacy policy.

To unlock Pro you paste the licence key from your Gumroad email on MapGauge's Pro licence page and click Activate. Only then does Chrome ask your permission for MapGauge to contact api.gumroad.com, and the only request is the licence check listed above. Pro unlocks features that use the same Google data described above (all categories, all loaded search results, category gap names, unlimited compares, lead filters); it reads nothing more from Google. The Free plan sends nothing to any server and keeps working without an account.
