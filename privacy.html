Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · applies to the MapGauge Chrome extension, Free plan

MapGauge Free sends nothing to any server. There are no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. Everything the extension does happens inside your own browser, and your audit reports stay on your own computer.

What MapGauge reads

MapGauge works only on Google Maps pages (https://www.google.com/maps/…). There it reads the data that Google Maps has already loaded for you:

  • Search results: the businesses in the results list of a search you made, to show badges with their category, rating and review count.
  • The business panel: the public details of a business you opened (name, categories, rating, review count, address, website, phone, opening hours, photo count, description, whether it is a hotel, and Google's IDs for it), to build the audit.

MapGauge sends no extra requests to Google. It makes no searches you didn't start, doesn't scroll or click for you, and never changes what Google shows. It doesn't read your Google account, your email, your location history or any other website.

While a Maps tab is open, this data is kept only in that tab's memory. It is gone when you close or reload the tab, unless you save an audit report.

What MapGauge stores

When you click Audit on a business, MapGauge saves an audit report. The toolbar popup shows your most recent reports from the same list.

  • What a report holds: the business's public details listed above, the time you saved it, and the address of the Google Maps page you were on (so you can reopen it). That address can include your search words and the map area you were looking at.
  • Where: in chrome.storage.local, the extension's own storage on your computer. It isn't synced to your Google account or sent anywhere.
  • How many: at most 50 reports. Auditing the same business again replaces its old report, and the oldest report is removed when a new one would go past 50.

How to delete them: remove the extension. Open chrome://extensions, find MapGauge and click Remove (or right-click the MapGauge icon in the toolbar and choose Remove from Chrome). Chrome then deletes all of MapGauge's saved data. If you install it again, it starts empty.

Sharing and selling

MapGauge collects no personal data, so there is nothing to sell, share or transfer. Nobody, including us, can see your reports.
