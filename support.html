Help

Something looks wrong on Google Maps

Click the MapGauge icon in Chrome’s toolbar and choose “Report a problem”. The self-check page shows which details MapGauge could read for the businesses you saw, and “Report a problem” there opens your own email app with MapGauge’s version and the details that weren’t read (no business names or searches). You choose whether to send it.

My sign-in code didn’t arrive

Check your spam folder. A code works once, for 10 minutes; “Send a new code” in the sign-in window sends another one. If you asked for several codes in a short time, the window says when you can try again.

I bought Pro but MapGauge still shows Free

Signing in doesn’t turn Pro on by itself. Sign in with the email you bought Pro with, open Restore Pro in the account window and click “Look up my purchase”, or enter the licence key from your Gumroad email. The purchase’s email must match your account’s. Still Free? Email us with the email you bought it with.

How do points and plans work?

See Pricing: what Free and Pro include, how grid scan points work, and how to cancel.

How do I cancel Pro?

On Gumroad, any time. Pro keeps working until the end of the month or year you paid for, then your account goes back to the Free plan.

How do I delete my account?

Email moklesur42@gmail.com from your account’s email address. What is deleted is in the privacy policy. Removing the extension deletes everything MapGauge keeps in your browser.
