Questions

How do I unlock Pro?

Buy Pro on Gumroad. In MapGauge, sign in with the email you bought it with: MapGauge emails you a 6-digit code, no password. Then open Restore Pro in the account window and click “Look up my purchase”, or enter the licence key from your Gumroad email. Signing in alone doesn’t turn Pro on, and the purchase’s email must match your account’s.

Do I need an account?

Only for Grid scan and Restore Pro. Audits, badges, compares, saved reports and the Rank log work without one and stay in your browser. A licence key pasted on MapGauge’s Pro page still unlocks the Pro features that run in your browser; Grid scan always uses your account.

How do grid scan points work?

One point is one search at one spot of the grid: a 5×5 scan uses 25 points, a 7×7 uses 49. Pro includes 800 points a month, monthly or yearly; they come back every month on the day you bought Pro, and unused points don’t carry over. Spots that couldn’t be checked give their points back. A free account gets one 5×5 scan instead.

Is there a free trial?

No, but the Free plan has no time limit, and a free account includes one grid scan, so you can try MapGauge as long as you like before you decide.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time on Gumroad. Pro keeps working until the end of the month or year you paid for, then your account goes back to the Free plan. Your saved reports and Rank log stay in your browser.

Who handles the payment?

Gumroad. MapGauge never sees your card details. What MapGauge sends and keeps for your account and Grid scan is in the privacy policy.
