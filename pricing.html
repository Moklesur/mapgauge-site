Questions

How do I unlock Pro?

Buy Pro on Gumroad and you get a licence key by email. Install MapGauge, click its icon in Chrome’s toolbar, choose “Activate Pro” and paste the key. Chrome asks once to let MapGauge contact Gumroad, only to check the key.

Do I need an account?

No. MapGauge has no accounts on either plan. Pro is unlocked by the licence key alone.

Is there a free trial?

No, but the Free plan has no time limit, so you can use the audit, badges and compares as long as you like before you decide.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time on Gumroad. Pro keeps working until the end of the period you paid for, then MapGauge goes back to the Free plan. Your saved reports stay.

Who handles the payment?

Gumroad. MapGauge never sees your card details, and the only thing Pro sends anywhere is the licence check described in the privacy policy.
